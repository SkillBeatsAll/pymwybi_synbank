# Syn Bank Share of Wallet Engine — Model Report

Methodology version `wallet-1.1.0`. 20 clients x 5 product pillars = 100 estimates. Every figure below is read back from `data/processed/*.parquet`; this file is generated by `analysis/wallet_model_report.py` and is not hand-edited.

## Methodology

Five product pillars, each with its own economic model and its own denominator. The pillars are never summed: the transactional and cross-border datasets overlap on SWIFT-channel rows by an amount the supplied fields cannot resolve.

For every client and pillar the engine separates **observed** activity (what is visible in Syn Bank's data), the **addressable wallet or opportunity** (what could reasonably be served), **share**, **gap**, and **confidence**. A gap is addressable business not observed in Syn Bank's supplied data. It is never described as confirmed competitor-held business.

| Pillar | Estimate basis | What the denominator is |
|---|---|---|
| Transactional / Cash Management | `addressable_cash_flow` | The client's whole disclosed operating payment-and-collection turnover, across all of its banks: revenue collected in plus cost of sales paid out. Share is the fraction of that flow Syn Bank currently handles. It is a client flow magnitude, never bank revenue -- the fee wallet on it is not estimable and is published as NULL. |
| FX / Global Markets | `peer_benchmark_addressable` | No disclosure states this client's total activity across all banks, so the wallet is the client's own economic driver scaled by the intensity a well-penetrated peer in this portfolio achieves. Share is penetration relative to that peer benchmark, not to a disclosed total. This understates true wallet where the whole portfolio is under-penetrated. |
| Trade Finance | `peer_benchmark_addressable` | No disclosure states this client's total activity across all banks, so the wallet is the client's own economic driver scaled by the intensity a well-penetrated peer in this portfolio achieves. Share is penetration relative to that peer benchmark, not to a disclosed total. This understates true wallet where the whole portfolio is under-penetrated. |
| Lending | `financing_opportunity` | A financing need indicator built from disclosed debt structure, not a claim about total lending wallet or about business a competitor holds. |
| Investment Banking / Capital Markets | `signal_only` | A ranked signal of mandate likelihood. No rand amount is estimated because the data cannot support one. |

> **The two bases are not comparable as rand.** Cash management is anchored on an accounting identity, so its wallet is the client's whole disclosed payment flow across all banks. FX and trade finance have no disclosed total, so their wallets are the client's own driver scaled by the intensity a well-penetrated peer achieves. Cash gaps are consequently orders of magnitude larger. The opportunity score ranks on within-pillar percentiles precisely so these bases are never compared directly.

## Formula

### 1. Transactional / Cash Management

```
wallet = collections + supplier_payments
       = revenue_total_zar x 1.0        (identity: revenue is collected into a bank)
       + cost_of_sales_zar x 1.0        (identity: cost of sales is paid out of one)
observed = domestic-channel collections + domestic-channel supplier payments (fiscal year)
```

Payroll, tax and intercompany sweeps are excluded from **both** sides. No employee-cost or tax-charge field exists, and treasury sweep volume has no external anchor; including observed sweeps against a denominator that cannot cover them would inflate share. The excluded activity is published as `out_of_scope_observed_zar`. Payroll survives as a *mandate signal* — instructions per employee — because observed payroll is a token R61m portfolio-wide at R11,048 an instruction.

### 2. FX / Global Markets

```
wallet = export_settlement + import_settlement + hedging_execution
       = foreign_revenue_basis x peer_P75(xb_inbound / foreign_revenue)
       + cost_of_sales_basis  x peer_P75(xb_outbound / cost_of_sales)
       + fx_forward_notional  x 1.0     (one roll, a deliberate floor)
observed = cross-border payment volume (fiscal year)
```

Foreign revenue is an **exposure** variable, never equated with settlement volume: MTN's Nigerian revenue is collected in naira inside Nigeria and never crosses a border. The exposure-to-volume conversion is measured from the client's peers -- the client itself is excluded from the population that sets its own coefficient. Directions are split so inbound and outbound demand cannot double count.

### 3. Trade Finance

```
wallet = import_documentary + export_documentary + guarantees
       = cost_of_sales_basis  x peer_P75(tf_import / cost_of_sales)     [suppressed: insurance, real_estate]
       + foreign_revenue_basis x peer_P75(tf_export / foreign_revenue)  [suppressed: insurance, real_estate]
       + revenue_total_zar     x peer_P75(tf_guarantees / revenue)      [all sectors]
observed = instruments dated in the fiscal year, all four statuses (annual issuance)
```

### 4. Lending

```
opportunity = refinancing + undrawn_facilities + working_capital + capex_funding
            = debt_current_zar        x 1.0   (structural: repayable within 12 months)
            + undrawn_facilities_zar  x 1.0   (structural: committed, unused headroom)
            + max(working_capital, 0) x median(debt_current / working_capital)
            + capex_zar               x 0.30  (JUDGEMENT)
share = NULL — Syn Bank's datasets contain no loan book
```

Gross debt is never used as the estimate: existing debt is not assumed replaceable. The output is named an *opportunity estimate*, not a wallet, and no share is claimed.

### 5. Investment Banking / Capital Markets

```
signal = 0.20 scale + 0.25 leverage + 0.25 near_term_maturity
       + 0.20 capex_intensity + 0.10 syndicate_breadth   (all percentile ranks)
estimate_zar = NULL — no rand amount is produced
```

A category is assigned only when a specific disclosure threshold is met, and `none_supported` is a legitimate and common answer.

## Assumptions

Every coefficient is declared in `src/syn_wallet/wallet/assumptions.py` with a basis saying where its authority comes from. Nothing numeric lives in the pillar models.

| Basis | Name | Value | Product | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| accounting_identity | collections_banked_share | 1.0 | cash_management | Revenue is ultimately received into a bank account, so the collections a corporate generates across all of its banks equals its revenue. This is an identity, not a coefficient to tune. Where reported revenue is not a cash measure (insurance), the sector applicability weight and a diagnostic flag carry the caveat instead of a fabricated haircut. |
| accounting_identity | supplier_payment_share_of_cogs | 1.0 | cash_management | Cost of sales is settled in cash to suppliers through a bank account. Timing differences shift the payment between periods but not the annual total in a steady state. |
| structural | benchmark_leave_one_out | 1.0 | all | Every peer benchmark excludes the client it is being used to estimate. Including a client in the population that sets its own coefficient is circular: a heavily penetrated client raises the benchmark it is then measured against, flattening its own apparent gap, and a client with no activity drags the benchmark down and makes its own share look healthy. With twenty clients a single one is 5% of the population and up to a third of its sector, so the circularity is material, not theoretical. |
| structural | fx_forward_rolls_per_year | 1.0 | fx_global_markets | A disclosed forward book has to be executed at least once to exist. Corporates typically roll shorter-dated hedges several times a year, but no forward tenor is disclosed, so one roll is used as a deliberate floor. |
| structural | refinancing_share_of_current_debt | 1.0 | lending | Debt classified as current is contractually repayable within twelve months. It must be repaid from cash or refinanced, so the whole balance is a financing decision inside the horizon. This is not a claim that Syn Bank could win it. |
| structural | undrawn_facility_share | 1.0 | lending | Undrawn committed facilities are, by disclosure, committed capacity another lender is already providing and the client is not using. The full balance is a contestable facility at its next renewal. |
| judgement | benchmark_min_sample | 4.0 | all | A portfolio benchmark needs at least four contributors, again after the estimated client is removed. Below four an upper-quartile intensity is an anecdote, and the coefficient is published as unavailable rather than as a number. |
| judgement | benchmark_percentile | 0.75 | all | Portfolio benchmarks are set at the 75th percentile of observed intensity, not the maximum. The maximum would let a single outlier define every client's wallet; the median would define the wallet as average performance and understate the opportunity. The upper quartile is 'what a well-penetrated peer achieves'. MODEL_SENSITIVITY.md measures what the median and the 80th percentile would do instead. |
| judgement | capex_debt_funded_share | 0.3 | lending | THE ONE UNDERIVED COEFFICIENT IN THE ENGINE. Capex is funded from a mix of operating cash flow and new debt, and no cash-flow statement field exists to split it. 0.30 is a deliberately conservative third. A diagnostic fires whenever this component exceeds half of a client's lending estimate, and the component breakdown lets a reviewer set it to zero. |
| judgement | confidence_band_high | 0.7 | all | HIGH band floor: every driver disclosed, identity-anchored, sector-applicable. |
| judgement | confidence_band_medium | 0.45 | all | MEDIUM band floor. Below it an estimate leans materially on imputation or on a proxy whose economic logic is weak for the sector. |
| judgement | ib_capex_intensity_threshold | 0.1 | investment_banking | A corporate finance / project funding category is only assigned above a 10% capex intensity, the level at which an investment programme is large enough to need external structuring. |
| judgement | ib_cost_of_debt_threshold | 0.09 | investment_banking | An implied blended cost of debt above 9% is elevated against the South African policy rate over the reporting window and supports a refinancing conversation. |
| judgement | ib_leverage_threshold | 0.5 | investment_banking | A refinancing or restructuring category needs leverage above half of annual revenue alongside an elevated cost of debt; below it the balance sheet does not indicate stress. |
| judgement | ib_near_term_maturity_threshold | 0.3 | investment_banking | A debt capital markets category is only assigned when at least 30% of gross debt is classified current. Below that, a near-term refinancing mandate is not supported by the disclosure. |
| judgement | opportunity_weight_confidence | 0.3 | all | Weight on model confidence. Large opportunities resting on imputed denominators are pushed down the ranking rather than silently promoted. |
| judgement | opportunity_weight_gap | 0.45 | all | Weight on the within-product percentile rank of the rand gap. Percentile rather than raw rand so that a client with a trillion-rand revenue base cannot dominate on scale alone, and so products with different estimate bases stay comparable. |
| judgement | opportunity_weight_headroom | 0.25 | all | Weight on commercial headroom (1 - share). A client where Syn Bank already handles most of the addressable activity is a retention conversation, not a growth opportunity. |
| judgement | sector_benchmark_min_sample | 3.0 | all | A sector benchmark is only formed from at least three peers, counted AFTER the client being estimated is removed. Below three, one peer's intensity would set the sector's frontier, and the estimate would be a restatement of a single company. Sectors that cannot reach three fall back to the portfolio benchmark, and the fallback reason is recorded per client rather than inferred. |
| judgement | share_cap | 1.0 | all | Reported share is capped at 100%. An uncapped share above 1.0 means observed activity exceeds the estimated wallet, which is a model finding, not a client fact; the uncapped value and a diagnostic flag are both retained. |

### Deliberate exclusions

| Name | Product | Why it is excluded |
|---|---|---|
| cash_management_fee_wallet | cash_management | The fee wallet on addressable cash flow is published as NULL for every client and is never derived. Syn Bank is fictional and discloses no pricing, so any rand fee figure would rest on an invented basis-point assumption. The flow figure is the client's turnover, not the bank's revenue, and the two are given different column names so a reader cannot mistake one for the other. |
| intercompany_rand_wallet | cash_management | DELIBERATELY EXCLUDED from both numerator and denominator. Treasury sweep volume has no external anchor, and including observed sweeps in the numerator while the denominator cannot cover them would inflate share. Reported separately as out-of-scope observed activity so the excluded amount stays visible. |
| opportunity_intensity_denominator | all | Opportunity intensity divides a product's rand gap by the client's OWN addressable cash flow (revenue + cost of sales). One denominator per client, identity-anchored and available for all twenty, so the five products share a common scale and the metric carries no fitted coefficient at all. It is a ratio, not a weighted index. |
| payroll_rand_wallet | cash_management | DELIBERATELY EXCLUDED from the rand wallet. No employee-cost field exists in the external data, and observed payroll volume is a token R61m across the whole portfolio for a year at an average of R11k per transaction. Sizing a payroll wallet would require inventing a cost per head. Payroll is carried instead as a mandate signal (rows per employee) feeding confidence and the narrative. |
| swift_overlap_treatment | cash_management | SWIFT-channel transactional rows conceptually overlap the cross-border pillar and the overlap cannot be resolved from the supplied fields. They are excluded from the cash numerator and NOT added to the FX numerator, so no rand is counted twice in either direction. The excluded amount is published per client. |
| tax_rand_wallet | cash_management | DELIBERATELY EXCLUDED. No tax charge is disclosed, and observed tax volume is R163m across the portfolio for a year on 5,395 instructions over three years. Retained as an engagement signal only. |
| trade_observed_scope | trade_finance | The trade numerator is the value of instruments dated inside the fiscal year across all four statuses, because the denominator is annual issuance demand. This is the one place the four statuses are legitimately summed, and it is stated rather than assumed. The live book (active + issued) is reported alongside it, never inside it. |

## Confidence

Four factors describe input quality and combine additively. A fifth — `evidence_directness`, how sound the *method* is — then **multiplies** that total, so having every input a model needs cannot make a weak model confident.

```
input_quality = 0.35 x input_completeness + 0.25 x sector_applicability + 0.20 x observation_support + 0.20 x internal_consistency
confidence    = input_quality x evidence_directness
```

| Coefficient basis | Directness |
|---|---|
| accounting_identity | 1.0 |
| structural | 0.9 |
| portfolio_benchmark | 0.6 |
| judgement | 0.35 |

Directness is scaled further by the fraction of expected components that could actually be built, and halved again when a wallet had to be floored at observed activity. Bands: HIGH at 0.70, MEDIUM at 0.45, LOW below.

> **A consequence worth stating.** FX and trade finance rest on peer benchmarks, whose directness is capped at 0.60, so **neither pillar can reach HIGH confidence by construction**. That is a real property of a model with no disclosed total to divide by, not an accident of calibration.

## Opportunity score

```
opportunity_score = 0.45 x gap_scale + 0.30 x confidence + 0.25 x headroom

gap_scale = percentile rank of the rand gap WITHIN its product
            (investment banking contributes its signal score instead)
headroom  = 1 - share, or a neutral 0.5 where no defensible share exists
```

Percentile rather than raw rand for two reasons: the five pillars have different estimate bases that must not be compared as one number, and rank is what stops a trillion-rand revenue base from dominating on scale alone. Confidence at 0.30 pushes large opportunities resting on imputed denominators down the ranking rather than silently promoting them.

## Limitations

**What this model does not claim.**

- It does not price anything. Syn Bank is fictional and has no disclosed pricing, so   every figure is a flow or balance magnitude, never bank revenue. There is no fee,   margin or basis-point assumption anywhere in the engine.
- It does not assert competitor ownership. A gap is addressable business not observed in   Syn Bank's supplied data. The absence of activity is not evidence that a competitor   holds it, and the presence of competitor-lending memos is evidence only that such   lending exists, not of its size.
- It does not claim a lending or investment-banking share. Syn Bank's datasets contain   no loan book and no deal pipeline, so both are reported with a NULL share and an   explicit reason.
- It does not sum the pillars. 279,389 SWIFT-channel transactional rows overlap the   cross-border dataset by an unresolvable amount. Those rows are excluded from the cash   numerator and not added to the FX numerator, so they are counted in neither; the   amount is published per client.
- It does not fit a model to the data. Every estimate is transparent arithmetic over   declared assumptions and can be recomputed by hand from the component breakdown.
- It does not impute a gap to zero. An undisclosed driver produces a NULL component and   a NULL share, never a confident zero.

**Where it is weakest.**

- Cash-management gap ranks correlate 0.986   with revenue ranks. The within-pillar ordering is close to a size ordering — the   honest consequence of an identity-anchored denominator, but it carries little   client-specific information beyond scale.
- FX and trade-finance wallets are peer-benchmark, not disclosed totals. If the whole   portfolio is under-penetrated, every wallet is understated together and no amount of   internal data would reveal it.
- `capex_debt_funded_share` at 0.30 is the one coefficient in the engine that could not   be derived. A diagnostic fires wherever it drives more than half a lending estimate.
- Four clients disclose no cost of sales and two sectors disclose none at all, so their   supplier-payment and import components rest on a peer ratio applied to revenue.
- Three clients fail the revenue-split identity and several disclose an unverifiable   zero debt; both are carried through as flags rather than corrected.
